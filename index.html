When a kitchen sink stops draining, the cause is usually deeper than the strainer basket. Years of cooking grease cool and harden inside the drain line, food scraps collect at the P-trap, and soap residue narrows the pipe a little more every month. Our Kitchen Drain Services

Kitchen Sink Unclogging

The core service: locating and clearing the blockage so the sink drains freely again, then testing the line with a full sink of water to confirm the clog is gone — not just pushed further down.

Grease Clog Removal

Grease is the number one cause of kitchen drain failure on the Treasure Coast. Hot water and detergent only move it a short distance before it cools and sticks again. Proper grease clog removal breaks up and clears the hardened layer from the pipe walls.

P-Trap Cleaning

The curved trap under the sink catches more than dropped rings — it collects food solids and grease until water can barely pass. Removing and cleaning the P-trap clears one of the most common clog points in the whole kitchen line.

Double Kitchen Sink Unclogging

When both bowls of a double sink back up together, the blockage sits past the point where the two drains join. Clearing it takes access from the right point so both sides drain evenly again.

Drain Snaking

For clogs beyond the trap, a drain snake (auger) reaches into the line to break through grease, food buildup and other obstructions that chemical cleaners cannot touch.

Signs Your Kitchen Sink Needs Professional Unclogging

  • Water drains slowly or pools in the sink
  • Gurgling sounds from the drain or nearby pipes
  • Bad smells coming from the sink, especially in warm weather
  • Water backs up into the second bowl of a double sink
  • The garbage disposal hums but the water does not move
  • The clog keeps coming back a few days after home remedies

A recurring clog is the clearest sign the problem is buildup inside the line rather than a single blockage — clearing it properly once is cheaper than fighting it every month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my kitchen sink keep clogging?

Repeated clogs usually mean grease and food residue have narrowed the inside of the drain line. Each small blockage clears, but the buildup stays and catches the next one faster. A full clearing of the line addresses the cause instead of the symptom.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner instead?

Chemical cleaners rarely dissolve hardened grease, and they can damage older pipes and the seals around the trap. They also leave the pipe walls coated, so the clog returns. Mechanical clearing removes the buildup itself.
